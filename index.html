Sustainability

Trade that respects the land, the grower and the future.

Sustainability is woven into how we source, ship and partner. We work hand-in-hand with farmers, cooperatives and certification bodies to ensure the commodities we trade meet rigorous environmental and social standards.

  • Traceable supply chains — from farm-gate to final delivery, with digital verification at every step.
  • Deforestation-free sourcing aligned with EUDR, RSPO and RTRS frameworks.
  • Smallholder partnerships investing in agronomy, financing and fair pricing for over 40,000 farmers.
  • Climate commitments with measurable scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions reduction by 2030.
